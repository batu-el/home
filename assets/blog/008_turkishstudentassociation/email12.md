Hi Onat,


First of all, I am glad that you sent these emails, because they allowed the community to see how problematic the process and the decisions have been, as well as what your true intentions are. As a team that has always tried to move TSA forward through the events we organized, we are saddened to see what this process has turned into after starting this journey with sincere intentions.


You may repeat as many times as you want that your decisions are in line with the Constitution, but you cannot legitimize this injustice through a Constitution narrative that you yourselves do not apply consistently.


The President recognized that the situation that emerged could be ethically questionable. For that reason, instead of deciding alone whether the members added to the mailing list at the last minute should be allowed to vote, he left the decision to the TSA community. If you truly believe that adding people suddenly was not wrong, then we do not understand why you avoided holding a democratic vote on this issue so strongly, to the point of ending the President's role.


Moreover, we do not see under what constitutional authority the decision made by the President serving at the time was invalidated in the Constitution that you seem to take into account only when it suits you.


To me, the most concerning point is that, unlike the person you accused of abusing his role and removed from office, the people currently making this decision include individuals who support a specific candidate, as well as a candidate himself. This is a conflict of interest. One cannot be both the plaintiff and the judge.


I do not think it is right for TSA's functioning and democratic processes to be damaged in this way for the sake of one election. If moving away from fairness and making unilateral decisions is considered grounds for removal, then we believe the same principles should be applied consistently to everyone. We suggest that you start with yourselves.


For this reason, I invite everyone to come vote, even if only to prevent this injustice from being allowed. I will not extend this further, but on behalf of everyone, we apologize that this election has reached such an uncomfortable point through emails and messages like this. We promise to follow the path of Batu, Alp, Barış, and many other past presidents who have led TSA in a fair and ethical way.


Cheers,

Gülbin, İpar, Melis, Alperen
