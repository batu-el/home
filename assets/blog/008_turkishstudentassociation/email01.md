Arkadaşlar / Dear friends,


This Sunday, we will hold our annual elections to elect the next board of the Stanford Turkish Student Association. We want this to be a fair, transparent, and welcoming process for everyone. We have outlined the details below, so you know what to expect on the election day.


**Location:** EVGR C134 Theater

**Date:** Sunday, May 31

**Time:** 4:30 PM – 6:30 PM (election & voting begins at 5:30 PM)


## how voting works

The election will be held anonymously through Google Forms, just like last year. Everyone eligible to vote will be given a code, and only this code will be used when voting. The confidentiality of your vote will be fully preserved.


## schedule

- 4:30 PM – 6:15 PM: Get your code to vote from check-in desk
- 4:45 PM – 5:15 PM: Candidate speeches (3 minutes per candidate, no back-and-forth)
- 5:30 PM: Election & Voting begins
- 6:15 PM: Election & Voting ends (no votes submitted after 6:15 PM will be accepted)
- 6:15 PM – 6:30 PM: Announcement of winners


## candidates

- **President:** Gülbin Atlı & Pelin Dedeler
- **Vice President:** Kutay Akın & İpar Kurt
- **Financial Officer:** Orhun Akengin & Alperen Konukbay
- **IT Administrator:** Melis Ata


You will not be choosing between two coalitions. You can, for example, vote for İpar as Vice President and Orhun as Financial Officer. According to the TSA Constitution, the election is conducted this way so that each position is decided independently.


If you have any questions before Sunday, you are always welcome to reach out. We look forward to seeing you there.


All the best,

Stanford Turkish Student Association


*P.S. We want to be fully transparent with our members about two issues that have come up. First, Senem's candidacy was announced after the candidacy declaration deadline. The Constitution has clear guidance on this matter; therefore, we are unable to accept that candidacy. Applying the rule consistently to everyone is the only way to keep the process fair. Second, it was brought to our attention that a large number of members were mass registered to the email list this past Sunday. We are looking into this carefully and will determine the appropriate course of action.*
