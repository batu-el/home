> **subject** · Decision on the Matter of the Last-Minute Bulk Registration of Voters in the Stanford Turkish Student Association Election


Dear Friends,


This past Sunday at 5:29 PM, exactly one week before the election, about 25 individuals were added to the official e-mail list in a single bulk action. To our understanding, these individuals did not subscribe one by one with a membership request submission. Their e-mail addresses were collected and added together by a current board member who holds administrative access to the membership system and who is also a candidate in this election. As President, I became aware of the action only after it had occurred. Because being a subscriber of the official e-mail list (together with being a registered Stanford student) is what determines who can vote, this bulk addition directly affects who is eligible in this election.


## considerations

After this was brought to my attention, I looked into it carefully and consulted Stanford's Office of Student Engagement, which oversees our organization.


**1. Scale relative to past elections.** To put the numbers in perspective, last year's presidential race had 40 votes in total and was decided 23–16–1. A bloc of 25 voters is therefore larger than both the margin of victory and the total vote count received by the winning candidate last year. At a comparable turnout, it could determine the outcome on its own. Therefore, the eligibility question alone could decide this election.


**2. Timing of the registrations.** The additions were completed one minute before the start of the 168-hour pre-election freeze, so on timing alone these subscriptions are not disqualified.


**3. Fairness and administrative access.** The additions were made by a board member who is also a candidate, using administrative access to the membership system. The constitution has no specific rule on a conflict of interest of this kind.


**4. Authority to administer membership and e-mail lists.** The constitution assigns administration of membership accounts and the email list to the President, Vice President, and IT Administrator. These duties are not among the listed responsibilities of the Financial Officer, who carried out the additions without constitutional authority. However, I should note that the Financial Officer has approved members for the mailing list in the past, so this rule has not always been strictly followed.


**5. Subsequent unsubscriptions.** Some of the people added unsubscribed themselves within hours of being registered. This potentially suggests that at least some of them did not intend to be registered in the first place, which raises a further question as to whether they had consented before being added.


**6. Manner of subscription.** The constitution frames joining as a subscription request that is then approved. Here, e-mail addresses were added in bulk rather than each person subscribing for themselves, which departs from the usual registration-and-approval process.


## decision

The timing requirement was technically met, but the manner in which these members were added and the authority under which the additions were made raise serious constitutional and fairness concerns. Nevertheless, on the central question of whether these individuals should be considered eligible voters, the constitution does not provide a clear-cut answer. I am not a candidate, and what matters most to me is that the result is one the entire community can trust, regardless of the outcome. So, we will decide it together, as part of the vote itself.


**Setup.** The election will be held anonymously through Google Forms, just like last year (note that in-person attendance is required). To preserve the confidentiality of your votes, everyone eligible to vote will be given a code, and only this code will be used when voting. A second category of voting codes will be provided to any of the last-minute-bulk-registered individuals who wish to participate.


**Eligibility Question.** In addition to the votes for the four Board positions, the ballot will include the following eligibility question: *"Should the members added through the bulk registration at 5:29 PM on Sunday be considered eligible voters in this election?"*


**Eligibility Decision.** For the eligibility question, votes cast by the last-minute-bulk-registered individuals will not be counted. The outcome of this question (decided by simple majority) will determine whether those individuals are considered eligible voters for the election. This will conclusively resolve the eligibility issue.


**Election Decision.** After the eligibility question is resolved, the votes for each Board position will be counted using the voter list determined above. The winners will then be announced based on those final counts.


Please come and make your voice heard.
