Selam Batu,


First, thank you for your efforts in running the election process.


I wanted to clarify the point about the recent email list additions. The TSA Constitution is very clear that membership and email list subscription requests only need to be postponed if they are submitted during the last 168 hours before the election.


I want to be direct here: presenting the pre-deadline additions as a questionable issue, while rejecting Senem's candidacy on constitutional grounds, creates an inconsistency. If we are applying the Constitution strictly in one case, then we should apply it strictly here as well. Since Senem's candidacy was not accepted because the candidacy deadline had passed, memberships approved before the 168-hour cutoff should likewise not be treated as irregular.


Opening this to further scrutiny despite the Constitution being clear risks making the process look selective rather than principled. That would be unfair to the current board, the candidates, and the members who joined on time.


There were also examples in last year's election, where you were elected and I was reelected, of people joining in the weeks prior to the cutoff deadline. This was not scrutinized then.


I would caution against framing the pre-deadline bulk subscription as an irregularity unless there is a specific constitutional violation being alleged. Otherwise, questioning it effectively questions the Constitution's own eligibility standard.
